# Data provenance

Both input tables ship inside the `geosimilarity` R package (version 3.9,
CRAN; authors Yongze Song and Wenbo Lyu). Nothing here is downloaded, and the
two CSVs in this directory are verbatim copies written by
`R/10-prepare-data.R` so that the inputs can be inspected without R.

| file | rows | columns | written from |
|---|---|---|---|
| `zn-samples.csv` | 894 | 12 | `data(zn, package = "geosimilarity")` |
| `grid-covariates.csv` | 13,132 | 12 | `data(grid, package = "geosimilarity")` |

## What they are

Geochemical Zn observations and a 1 km prediction grid for the Leonora mining
region, Western Australia — the Zn subset of the case study in

> Song, Y. (2022). Geographically Optimal Similarity.
> *Mathematical Geosciences* 55, 295-320. doi:10.1007/s11004-022-10036-8

The samples come from the Geological Survey of Western Australia (GSWA)
Geochemistry / WACHEM database; observations sharing a location were averaged
before the data were packaged. Columns:

- `zn-samples.csv`: `Lon`, `Lat`, `Zn` (ppm), then the nine covariates
  `Elevation`, `Slope`, `Aspect` (terrain, from the Australian Smoothed DEM),
  `Water` (distance to rivers and lakes), `NDVI` (MODIS MOD13Q1.006, 2010
  annual mean), `SOC` and `pH` (Soil and Landscape Grid of Australia), `Road`
  (distance to state and local roads) and `Mine` (distance to mining sites).
- `grid-covariates.csv`: `GridID`, `Lon`, `Lat` and the same nine covariates,
  resampled to the 1 km prediction lattice.

Distances are in kilometres, NDVI and pH are dimensionless, SOC is a mass
fraction, Slope and Aspect are in degrees, Elevation in metres.

## What is not here

The paper also builds two geological covariates for Zn — *distance to
Zn-related lithology* and *distance to Zn-related faults*, derived from the
Surface Geology of Australia 1:1M data set — and they are the two strongest
predictors in its Table 2. They are **not** part of the packaged data, and the
packaged sample count (894) is smaller than the paper's 966 Zn samples. See
`env/requirements.md` and `NOTES-for-tutorial.md` for what that means for the
numbers.

## Derived files

`data/derived/` holds the intermediates each step hands to the next; all are
regenerated by `Rscript run-all.R` and none is an input.

| file | written by | contents |
|---|---|---|
| `samples.rds` | step 10 | screened samples (885 rows), all samples (894), the grid, the dropped row indices |
| `selected-vars.rds` | step 20 | the selected covariates, the drops, the final VIFs, the correlation table |
| `best-kappa.rds` | step 30 | lambda and the kappa-RMSE curve for the screened data |
| `best-kappa-unscreened.rds` | step 50 | lambda and the curve for the unscreened robustness run |
